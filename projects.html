Projects & Ventures

Building cultural experiences across Northern Norway

Kulturbryggeriet & Nord på Hjul

Founded 2022

Mobile roller-skating disco events bringing joy and community connection across 8 municipalities in Northern Norway. Over 500 participants engaged through 20+ events, creating unique cultural experiences in local communities.

Elvic Gruppen AS

Founded 2024

Hospitality and tourism development company supporting regional economic growth in Northern Norway. Focused on creating sustainable tourism experiences that benefit local communities.

Elvic Adventures

Founded 2024

Water biking and outdoor tourism experiences making Northern Norway's stunning nature accessible to all. Providing unique ways to explore the region's fjords, lakes, and coastal areas.

Musikkbryggeriet

Founded 2017

Music industry development program connecting emerging artists with professionals through events, workshops, and mentorship. Supporting the next generation of Norwegian musicians in finding their voice and audience.

Impact

  • 500+ participants engaged across cultural events
  • 8 municipalities reached in Northern Norway
  • 20+ community events organized
  • Featured in 32+ publications
  • Multiple emerging artists mentored through Musikkbryggeriet
