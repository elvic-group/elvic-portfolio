About Me

My Journey

Born in DR Congo and raised in Norway, I've spent over a decade bridging cultures through music, entrepreneurship, and community development. My journey began at Notam music studio in Oslo, where I discovered the power of music to connect people across backgrounds.

From performing at major festivals like Moldejazz, Slottsfjell, and the 2016 Youth Olympics, I transitioned into cultural entrepreneurship, founding ventures that bring communities together through shared experiences.

Music Career

As a guitarist and music producer, I've collaborated with artists across genres, blending African rhythms with Nordic influences. My music has been featured in various publications and performances throughout Norway.

Through Musikkbryggeriet, I continue to support emerging artists, providing mentorship, workshops, and performance opportunities to the next generation of musicians.

Entrepreneurship

I believe in creating experiences that bring joy and connection to communities. My ventures focus on accessible tourism, cultural events, and community-building activities in Northern Norway.

From roller-skating disco events to water biking adventures, each project is designed to make Northern Norway's unique culture and nature accessible to everyone.

Recognition

Featured in 32+ publications including iFinnmark, Nordlys, Hammerfestingen, and national outlets. My work has been recognized for its impact on regional development and cultural accessibility in Northern Norway.
